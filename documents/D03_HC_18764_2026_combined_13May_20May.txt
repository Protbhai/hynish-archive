10. This Court finds that the objection relating to the maintainability of 
the reference under Section 25 (1) of the Act, 1860 was specifically raised 
by the petitioners and it was also recorded in the impugned order; but the 
impugned order has been passed without adverting to the requirements of 
Section 25(1) of the Act 1860 and as desired by this court in its order 
passed in Writ-C No. 17195 of 2025, dated 01.08.2025; and further the 
manner in which the elections held on 22.03.2025 has been discarded, 
shocks the conscious of this Court, as any subsequent event cannot 
invalidate an election, which was held in the year 2025; and as such by 
WRIC No. 18764 of 2026
3
non-consideration of the grounds taken by the petitioner, the impugned 
order is liable to be set aside on the grounds of violation of principles of 
natural justice and the same being absolutely perverse.
11. The court is also conscious of the fact that there is an alternative 
remedy provided under Section 25 (1) (d) of the Act, 1860, but in view of 
the admission of the learned Standing Counsel and learned counsel 
appearing on behalf of respondent no.4 that the issues raised was never 
adverted to by the Prescribed Authority, this Court finds that in the given 
circumstances, the present writ petition cannot be dismissed on the 
ground of alternative remedy, as there is admitted violation of the 
principles of natural justice. Accordingly, the order dated 24.04.2026 is 
hereby set aside.
12. I have also perused the impugned order dated 29.04.2026 and the 
Deputy Registrar has merely observed that since the election dated 
22.03.2026 has been set aside and the elections have also not taken place, 
while the term of Committee of Management has expired, therefore, 
elections can be held, invoking the provisions of 25 (2) of the Act, 1860.
13. At this juncture, Shri Gautam Baghel, Advocate has relied upon 
Clause 26 of the model Bye-laws of the society. For the sake of 
convenience, the same is reproduced below:
“26. Election and term of office -
(i) The annual general meeting of the Association, to be held in the 
first quarter of each financial year, shall elect neither less than 4, nor 
more than 10 Members including the President, Vice President, 
Secretary and Treasurer from M embers of the Association. Provided 
that no member shall be eligible for election if he has been found guilty 
of misusing the post during his previous term. The term of office of the 
office-bearers of the Board shall be one year from the date of assuming 
office and they will be eligible for re-election. Provided that a person 
can not hold the same post continuously for more than 2 years.
•
(ii) An arrangement shall be made by the board to ensure that one 
third of members of the board retire annually.
•
(iii) The office bearers shall hold office until their successors have •

been elected and hold their first meeting.”
14. Perusal of the aforesaid bye-laws, which has been duly approved by 
the Authority concerned; specifically under Clause 26 (iii), it has been 
provided that the office bearers shall hold office until their successors 
have been elected and hold their first meeting. Since the elections were 
notified on 21.02.2026, but could not be held on account of an order dated 
23.02.2026; it cannot be said that the elections have not been proceeded 
with and therefore, now the election is to be held invoking the provisions 
of Section 25 (2) of the Act, 1860.
15. In view of the aforesaid, this Court finds it appropriate to relegate the 
matter to the Prescribed Authority to examine whether reference which 
was made under Section 25 (1) of the Act, 1860 was made in accordance 
with law, and to proceed only in case it is found that the reference was 
made to it by at least 1/4th members of the Society.
16. The aforesaid decision is to be taken within a period of one month, 
from the date of production of a certified copy of this order; whereafter 
the election of the Committee of Management shall be held accordingly, 
within a period of one month from the date of decision of the Prescribed 
Authority, under Section 25(1) of the Act, 1860. 
17. With the aforesaid observations and directions, the writ petition stands 
disposed off.
