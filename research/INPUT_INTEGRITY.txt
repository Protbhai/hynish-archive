HOW THE RECORDS WERE CHECKED
All 59 files under records/ and documents/ are byte-identical (SHA-256) to the copies collected for the working archive.
Records under records/ are copies of documents the builder filed publicly on UP RERA, association notices published on phaoa.in, and statutes/judgments.
Records under records/06_official_records/ are official documents (a High Court judgment, Authority minutes, an election officer's letter, the Deed of Declaration and NPCL notices) that reached residents through WhatsApp; each was read in full, and official records that name private residents are cited in the source register but not copied here.
Court files under documents/ come from the Allahabad High Court portal or a public judgment mirror (marked as such).
Scanned official records were read page by page where the archive relies on them (review label "Key contents reviewed").
Identical bytes prove an unchanged copy, not authenticity or legal correctness.
